# Two decks: for accounting firms, and for companies

## Context
The public deck (`/deck`, cycle `2026-10-03-product-deck.md`) is one 14-slide pitch written before the three-stage spine
([ADR 0014](../adrs/0014-three-stage-spine.md)), Papan kantor, Kelengkapan data, the client upload link, Pajak Masa, Ekualisasi PPN
and Tanya Buku existed. It also breaks the repo's own look rules (aurora background, shiny and gradient text, glow; see
`ui-rules` → "No AI-slop"). The owner asked for a new version split by audience:

1. **Accounting firms managing several clients** — the buyer in ADR 0014.
2. **Companies that need accounting software** — a buyer ADR 0014 only reaches *through* firms. See Assumption 1.

Brief from the owner: simple, rich in visuals and illustrations that carry the product's value, no nonsense, no typical AI-slop,
Bahasa Indonesia, reviewed and iterated before it is shown.

## Spec
Approval: the owner's brief, given in-session, is the spec. It names audience, tone, language and the quality bar. No new
dependency, schema or app route is added.

- [ ] **Two decks, static, public, no login:** `/deck/kantor` (firms) and `/deck/perusahaan` (companies); `/deck` is a two-link chooser.
- [ ] **Same look as the product** (`ui-rules`): light canvas, navy ink, one blue, hairlines, sharp corners, mono micro-labels, flat
  navy for cover and close. No gradients, glow, shiny text, glass, emoji or decorative motion. Motion limited to a 200 ms slide fade;
  none under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- [ ] **Every slide carries one picture that shows the mechanism:** product mockups in HTML/CSS and diagrams in inline SVG, built from
  synthetic data and labelled *data demo sintetis*. No stock art, no real client, no bank logos.
- [ ] **Honest content:** only what Buku does today (README, ADRs, cycle docs). No invented metrics, customers, prices or time-saved
  figures. Each deck has a slide that says what Buku does not do. The success measure offered is the one Buku itself records
  (`npm run close:timeline`: file received → locked → report sent).
- [ ] **Presenting and reading:** 16:9 stage with arrows / space / swipe / `#n` deep links / `F`; below 900 px or in portrait it reads as one
  scroll. `@media print` gives one slide per landscape page so a PDF can be exported.
- [ ] `/deck` stays public and same-origin only (CSP unchanged).

**Non-goals:** app code, pricing, contact forms, analytics, a CMS, bilingual copy, naming or ranking competitors, committing PDFs.

**Assumptions** (owner to confirm):
1. ADR 0014 default *"the buyer is the accounting firm, and SMEs are reached through firms"* is not reversed. The company deck therefore
   sells what a company gets from Buku today — books closed from its own statements, by its in-house accountant or its firm — and says
   plainly that Buku is not an invoicing, POS or payroll tool. A company reaches Buku only by invitation, as now.
2. The old product deck is replaced, not kept beside the new ones. `docs/cycles/2026-10-03-product-deck.md` remains as history.
3. Right Jet's logo, email and colours are still unavailable: text wordmark and `right-jet.com` only.
4. Names in mockups come from the synthetic demo firm (*KJA Demo & Rekan*, Grup Ayam Nusantara, CV Sinar Retail) plus clearly fictional
   extras. Accurate and Jurnal are named only as sources Buku can import from, as the README does.
