# Pajak Masa: PPh 25 angsuran

## Context
Every PT on the normal regime pays PPh 25 monthly by the 15th. The amount is set by last year's SPT and changes from the masa that SPT is
filed, or with an SKP or pembetulan. Buku read PPh 25 bank lines only as year-end credits on Pajak Badan. Pajak Masa listed PPN, PPh 21,
23 and the other withholdings, but never PPh 25. A missed or short PPh 25 instalment, which costs the client interest under Pasal 9(2a) KUP,
went unseen until December. An Indonesian accountant checks it every month alongside the rest.

## Spec
- [x] **Angsuran, effective-dated:** `TaxInstalment` (entity, `from` = first day of a masa, `amount` ≥ 0, unique per entity and masa).
  The instalment of a masa is the latest one from on or before it. Set and removed on Pajak Masa (admin and akuntan), audited (`PPH25`).
- [x] **`masaReport.pph25`** for a normal-regime company (none under PP 55, nor without any instalment or PPh 25 payment):
  - the previous masa (due the 15th of the report month), judged like the other rows: Nihil / Lunas / Lebih setor / Disetor terlambat /
    Kurang setor / Belum jatuh tempo;
  - the report masa (due the 15th of the next month).
  - Payments are bank lines tagged PPH_25 (out, reviewed), by masa pajak (`taxMonth`, else the month before payment, rule 5d).
  - With payments but no instalment, the masa reads *Disetor* and a note asks for the instalment.
- [x] **Pajak Masa:** a PPh 25 row in *Setoran per jenis pajak* (angsuran, due date, the masa before and its state, status). Under it,
  the instalment in force and a small form: *masa mulai* + *angsuran per bulan*.
- [x] **Tutup Buku** *Pajak masa disetor* counts PPh 25 like the other rows (REVIEW when short or late).
- [x] **Excel kertas kerja:** the PPh 25 row on Ringkasan.
- [x] accounting-rules 5j; README.

**Non-goals:** computing the instalment from the SPT (the accountant types it; next year's estimate is already on Pajak Badan);
PPh 25 for new taxpayers, banks or BUMN (special formulas); the demo story (no instalment seeded, so the row stays hidden there).
**Gate-reopeners:** one migration (new table `TaxInstalment`).
