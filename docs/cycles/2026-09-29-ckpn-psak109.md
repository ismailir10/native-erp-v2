# CKPN piutang (PSAK 109) — matriks provisi dari umur piutang

## Context
Second of the five Balancio-gap cycles (approved "all in that order"). Balancio's *Kalkulator CKPN PSAK 109* takes monthly aging CSVs and
computes roll rates, loss rates per bucket, forward-looking scenarios and the adjusting journal. Buku already knows every invoice and when
it was paid (Piutang & Utang), so it can build the aging at any past month-end itself — no CSV — and post the allowance through the GL,
with the tax side (koreksi fiskal, deferred tax) following automatically.

## Spec
- [x] **Setting per entity** (*CKPN piutang usaha*): method **Roll rate** (from Buku's own month-end aging, last N months, default 12) or
      **Manual** (a loss rate per bucket typed by the accountant); the loss rate of the last bucket (> 90 hari, default 100 %); a
      **forward-looking** factor (default 100 %, 0–300 %). Rates are basis points (not money).
- [x] **Roll rates** from month-end aging snapshots of the entity's sales invoices (open amount by bucket, the same aging as the page),
      invoice by invoice: of what was open in b at month t and has aged beyond b by month t+1, the share still open then, capped at 100 %,
      averaged over the months where b had such an amount;
      **loss rate** of a bucket = the last bucket's loss rate × the product of the roll rates from it to the last bucket. With fewer than two
      snapshots the roll-rate method says so and proposes nothing.
- [x] **CKPN** = Σ open amount per bucket × loss rate × forward-looking factor, rounded half up per bucket, at the period end.
- [x] **Journal by click** (ADJUSTMENT, dated the period end): brings **1135 Cadangan Kerugian Penurunan Nilai Piutang** (contra receivable,
      credit balance) to the CKPN against **6185 Beban Kerugian Penurunan Nilai Piutang** (increase: Dr 6185 / Cr 1135; release: the reverse).
      The difference is taken against the account's GL balance at the period end, so anything else that moved 1135 is respected. Accounts join
      the COA template; an existing client gets them on the first posting when the codes are free (else refused by name).
- [x] **Tax:** 6185's name matches the *cadangan / CKPN* correction category (already suggested as a positive beda waktu); the tax pack's
      deferred tax now also counts the **allowance** (1135 credit balance) as a deductible temporary difference (DTA 22 %).
- [x] **Close control** `ckpn:<entity>` (only once the entity has a CKPN setting): REVIEW while the journal has a difference.
- [x] **UI:** a *CKPN (PSAK 109)* card on the Piutang tab: method, the matrix (bucket · saldo · roll rate · loss rate · CKPN), forward-looking
      factor, GL balance of 1135, the proposed journal with *Catat*, and the snapshot months used. Works at 390 px.

**Gate-reopeners (flagged):** schema migration (CkpnSetting; CHECKs on basis points); COA template gains 1135, 6185; accounting rule 5e; the tax
pack's deferred tax changes (allowance included). No dependency, no AI.

**Non-goals:** the general approach (stages, PD × LGD, SICR); writing off an invoice (hapus buku) — a manual journal for now; payables;
scenario sets beyond one factor; CSV import of external aging history.

**Assumptions:**
1. Month-end snapshots come from Buku's invoices and settlements; months before the first invoice don't count.
2. Receivables use the five buckets of the aging (belum jatuh tempo, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, > 90).
3. The allowance is a deductible temporary difference in full (Pasal 9(1)c: provisions aren't deductible until written off).

## Implementation
- Plan: T1–T5 sequential, inline; scope pre-approved with the five-cycle plan. T2 and T3 landed in one commit (the computation and its posting share `lib/receivables/ckpn.ts`).
- **Refinement of the spec (not a shape change):** roll rates are computed invoice by invoice instead of bucket totals. A bucket total at t+1
  mixes items that were already there (> 90 hari accumulates) and items that haven't aged yet; following each invoice avoids both. A
  bucket with an open amount and no history blocks the proposal instead of guessing.
- T1: `CkpnSetting` + enum `CkpnMethod`, migration `20260929040000_ckpn` (CHECKs on bp, factor, months); COA 1135 (contra, credit) and 6185;
  mapping sends allowance source accounts to 1135 when present and impairment expense to 6185; 1135 can't be an invoice's receivable account.
- T2/T3: `lib/receivables/ckpn.ts` (agingAt, rollRates, lossRates, allowance, ckpn, saveCkpnSetting, postCkpn, ckpnView); `lib/coa/ensure.ts`
  (template accounts on first use, moved from the tax post); control `ckpn:`; tax pack deferred = assets + allowance (page and workpaper
  show both). Tests `tests/unit/ckpn.test.ts`, `tests/db/ckpn.test.ts`.
- T4: `components/app/ckpn-card.tsx` on the Piutang tab; `saveCkpnSettingAction`, `postCkpnAction`; `e2e/ckpn.spec.ts`.
- T5: `accounting-rules` 5e (+ 5d deferred), README.
