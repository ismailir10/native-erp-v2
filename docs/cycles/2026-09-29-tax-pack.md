# Paket Pajak Badan — koreksi fiskal, PPh badan, pajak kini & tangguhan

## Context
Feedback 2026-09-28 (Syaukani): "tambahin beberapa perhitungan penerapan PSAK … perhitungan tax, depresiasi dsb". Depreciation shipped
(cycle 2026-09-28-fixed-asset-register, with the fiscal estimate and book − fiscal difference per asset). The tax side is still the
"Pajak bulan ini" card: PPN and PPh 4(2)/21 totals from bank lines, labelled *not an SPT*. What a firm prepares at year end (and
estimates during the year) for every PT client is missing: **rekonsiliasi fiskal** (commercial profit → koreksi fiskal positif/negatif →
PKP), **PPh badan terutang** (Pasal 17 at 22 %, the Pasal 31E reduction for turnover ≤ Rp 50 M, or the PP 55/2022 final 0,5 % regime),
**kredit pajak** (PPh 25 paid, PPh 22/23 withheld by others) → **PPh 29 kurang bayar / 28A lebih bayar**, the next year's PPh 25, and
the **journal**: current tax (pajak kini) and — for entities reporting under SAK EP — deferred tax (pajak tangguhan) on temporary differences.

Found while scoping: the firm-wide rule sends bank lines "PPH 25" to **8100 Beban Pajak Penghasilan** (an expense), although an
instalment is a prepayment credited against the year's tax. The pack needs it as a credit.

Outcome: a *Pajak Badan* page per entity and fiscal year that computes, from the ledger, the whole path from laba komersial to PPh 29,
cites where every figure comes from, and posts the tax journals by the accountant's click. An estimate for the accountant's working
papers — never an SPT, never filed.

## Spec
Fiscal reconciliation (`lib/tax/`, read at request time; nothing computed is stored)
- [ ] **Fiscal year = calendar year**, per entity with IDR books, computed **through the selected month** (year to date; at December,
      the year). Starting point: *laba sebelum pajak* of the entity's Laba Rugi for the year to date (tax accounts BEBAN_PAJAK excluded,
      as the report already does).
- [ ] **Automatic koreksi fiskal** (deterministic, each citing its source and linking to it):
      – **Penyusutan**: book − fiscal depreciation of the year from the fixed-asset register (positive when book > fiscal) — beda waktu;
      – **Penghasilan yang dikenai PPh final**: revenue on accounts whose bank lines carry PPh 4(2) (interest / jasa giro, 4900) comes out
        (negative) — beda tetap;
      – **Beban yang tidak dapat dikurangkan by account word** (*sumbangan, donasi, denda, sanksi, bunga penagihan, natura, kenikmatan,
        entertain/jamuan tanpa daftar nominatif*): **suggested only**, with the account and its amount — the accountant accepts it (it
        becomes a manual correction) or dismisses it.
- [ ] **Manual koreksi fiskal** per entity-year: description, positive/negative, beda tetap/waktu, amount (typed, rule 6), optional account
      reference; editable and deletable while the year's December is open.
- [ ] **PKP** = laba sebelum pajak + positive − negative corrections, rounded **down to full thousands** (a loss gives PKP 0; fiscal loss
      carry-forward is a non-goal, stated on screen).
- [ ] **Regime per entity-year**: *Normal* (default) or *PP 55/2022 final 0,5 %* (chosen by the accountant; Buku doesn't judge eligibility,
      it shows the rule: turnover ≤ Rp 4,8 M and the time limit). Normal: **22 %**; with **turnover (peredaran bruto = usaha revenue, year
      to date) ≤ Rp 50 M**, Pasal 31E: the part of PKP from turnover up to Rp 4,8 M is taxed at 11 % (PKP × 4,8 M ÷ turnover, the whole PKP
      when turnover ≤ 4,8 M), the rest at 22 %; each part rounded down to Rupiah. Final regime: 0,5 % × turnover, no corrections, no credits.
- [ ] **Kredit pajak**: PPh 25 paid (bank lines tagged PPh 25, dated in the year, whatever account they sit on) + manual credits (PPh 22 /
      23 / 24 withheld by others: type, bukti potong number, date, amount, the account it is recorded on). **PPh 29** = terutang − credits
      (negative = lebih bayar, Pasal 28A). **PPh 25 next year** (Pasal 25 basic formula) = (terutang − PPh 22/23/24 credits) ÷ 12, rounded down.
- [ ] **Deferred tax (pajak tangguhan, SAK EP)** — shown for entities with registered assets: temporary difference = fiscal book value −
      book value of the register's assets at the period end; deferred tax asset (liability when negative) = difference × 22 %.
- [ ] **Journals by click** through `postJournal()` (kind ADJUSTMENT, dated the period end; locked months refuse): *pajak kini* moves the books
      to the computed position — Dr 8100 current tax expense, Cr the accounts the credits sit on (1180 …; credits already expensed on 8100
      reduce the 8100 debit instead), Cr **2146 Utang PPh Pasal 29** (or Dr **1181 PPh Lebih Bayar** when negative); *pajak tangguhan* moves
      **1270 Aset Pajak Tangguhan** / **2320 Liabilitas Pajak Tangguhan** to the computed balance against **8110 Beban (Manfaat) Pajak
      Tangguhan**. Each post books only the **difference** from what earlier tax postings of that year already booked, so re-posting after
      a correction adjusts instead of doubling; nothing to post = no button. Postings are listed with their entries.
- [ ] **Firm rule fix:** bank lines "PPH 25" go to **1180 Pajak Dibayar di Muka** (tag PPh 25) instead of 8100. Existing lines are not
      reclassified (history stays); the pack handles both placements.
- [ ] **Close control** `tax:<entity>` (REVIEW, only in December, only for PT/CV entities with IDR books): "PPh badan tahun ini belum dijurnal"
      while the current-tax proposal has a non-zero difference; acknowledgeable like any REVIEW.
- [ ] **UI:** *Pajak Badan* page under Akuntansi (ScopeBar period + entity; entity required): NextStep; the reconciliation as a statement
      (laba komersial → each correction with its source link → PKP → tax by bracket → credits → PPh 29 / 28A → PPh 25 next year), suggestions
      with Terima/Abaikan, manual corrections and credits with add/delete, regime switch, deferred tax, the two proposals with *Catat*, and
      the postings. Every figure labelled **estimasi — bukan SPT**. Works at 390 px.

**Gate-reopeners (flagged):** schema migration (TaxYear — entity, year, regime; FiscalCorrection; TaxCredit; TaxPosting — entity-year, kind
CURRENT/DEFERRED, entry; dismissed suggestions; CHECKs amount > 0); COA template gains **1181, 1270, 2146, 2320, 8110** (existing clients get
them on first posting when the code is free, else the accountant picks, as with 7300); a **firm rule change** (PPH 25 → 1180); new accounting
rule **5d**. No dependency, no AI.

**Non-goals:** filing / e-Bupot / Coretax export; SPT 1771 layout; fiscal loss carry-forward (kompensasi kerugian); non-calendar fiscal years;
PPh badan for individuals (PERORANGAN entities: SPT 1770 is different) and non-IDR entities; deferred tax on anything but the fixed-asset
register (e.g. employee benefits, provisions); tax rate changes by year (22 % fixed, one constant); annualising a year-to-date turnover for
31E; eligibility checks for PP 55/2022.

**Assumptions:**
1. Turnover (peredaran bruto) = the entity's PENDAPATAN_USAHA revenue in the year to date; *pendapatan lain* is not turnover.
2. The PPh 4(2) correction takes the whole balance of the revenue accounts whose bank lines carry a PPh 4(2) tag in the year (interest and
   jasa giro); other final-taxed income (e.g. rent) is a manual correction.
3. Deferred tax uses 22 % on the whole temporary difference (the 31E reduction is not applied to deferred tax).
4. The pack is per entity; a group's entities are computed separately (no fiscal consolidation exists).
5. A correction or credit belongs to an entity-year; after the year's December is locked, they are read-only.
