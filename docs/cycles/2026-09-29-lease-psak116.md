# Sewa (PSAK 116) — daftar sewa, aset hak guna dan liabilitas sewa

## Context
Third of the five Balancio-gap cycles (approved "all in that order"). Balancio's *Kalkulator PSAK 116* turns a lease contract (payments,
term, discount rate) into the right-of-use asset, the lease liability with its amortisation table, the current / non-current split and the
journals. Buku has an asset register (PSAK 16) but a lease is still booked as rent: no ROU asset, no liability, no interest. The lease
register adds that, posts through the GL, and feeds the tax pack (the tax law ignores PSAK 116: rent is deducted, not depreciation + interest).

## Spec
- [ ] **Lease register per entity** (*Sewa (PSAK 116)* page, next to Aset Tetap): name, lessor, start month, term in months (> 12), payment
      amount, payment interval (monthly / quarterly / semi-annual / annual; the term a multiple of it), paid in advance or in arrears, and the
      annual discount rate (incremental borrowing rate, basis points). Short-term leases (≤ 12 months) are refused with the reason
      (recognition exemption: rent straight to expense).
- [ ] **Schedule** (computed, never stored): lease liability at commencement = present value of the payments at the monthly rate
      (annual ÷ 12), exact (rational bigint), rounded half up once. Per month: payment (advance: at the start, arrears: at the end), interest
      = liability × monthly rate rounded half up, closing liability; the rounding residue goes to the last interest month so the liability
      ends at 0. ROU = the initial liability, depreciated straight line over the term (⌊ROU ÷ term⌋, the last month the rest). Current portion
      at a month-end = liability then − liability 12 months later; non-current = the latter.
- [ ] **Journals** (`ADJUSTMENT`, by the accountant's click, each keeps its lease as source):
      commencement (on registration, dated the start month's first day): Dr **1230 Aset Hak Guna** / Cr **2170 Liabilitas Sewa Jangka Pendek**
      (current part) / Cr **2400 Liabilitas Sewa Jangka Panjang**; monthly (dated the month-end, one entry per lease-month, *Catat jurnal sewa*
      posts every month due up to the selected month): Dr **6181 Beban Penyusutan Aset Hak Guna** / Cr **1239 Akumulasi Penyusutan Aset Hak
      Guna**, Dr **7195 Beban Bunga Liabilitas Sewa** / Cr 2170, and the reclass of the non-current decrease Dr 2400 / Cr 2170. Payments come from
      the bank statement, classified in Review to 2170. Accounts join the COA template; existing clients get them on first use when free.
- [ ] **Close control** `lease:<entity>`: REVIEW while a monthly journal is due and not posted, or the GL of 1230 / 1239 / 2170 + 2400
      differs from the register at the month-end (the usual reason: a payment not yet classified to 2170).
- [ ] **Tax pack:** an automatic temporary correction *Sewa (PSAK 116)* = (ROU depreciation + interest) − fiscal rent for the year to date,
      where fiscal rent = the total payments straight line over the term; the deferred tax gains the lease's temporary difference = (payments to
      date − fiscal rent to date) − (ROU carrying amount − liability).
- [ ] **Cancelling** a lease registered by mistake: only while none of its monthly journals is posted; a reversing entry of its commencement
      journal (the ledger is append-only), dated like it (so its month must be open); the lease stays listed as *dibatalkan*.
- [ ] **Hardening found while designing:** ledger-import *new account* codes skip every COA template code (so 1135, 1181, 2146, 2320 … can't be
      taken by an imported account), and a template account found on the client must also have the template's normal balance and name
      (compared case- and space-insensitively), else it is refused by name.

**Gate-reopeners (flagged):** schema migration (Lease, LeasePosting, enum LeaseTiming; CHECKs); COA template gains 1230, 1239, 2170, 2400, 6181,
7195; accounting rule 5f; the tax pack gains a correction source and deferred component. No dependency, no AI.

**Non-goals:** lease modifications, remeasurement (index, extension), early termination; initial direct costs, incentives, restoration
provisions; variable payments; lessor accounting; foreign-currency leases (entity currency only); mid-month commencement (the start month
counts in full); transition journals for leases already in the opening balances.

**Assumptions:**
1. Monthly compounding at the nominal annual rate ÷ 12; the start month is month 1 of the term.
2. A lease already running when the books start is registered from the first month in Buku with its remaining payments and term (the
   modified retrospective approach: ROU = liability at that date).
3. Fiscal rent: payments deducted straight line over the lease term (prepaid rent spread over its period); PPh 23 / 4(2) withholding is the
   payment's business, not the register's.
