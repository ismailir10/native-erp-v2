# PPh 21: an employee's last masa when they leave mid-year

## Context
PMK 168/2023 recomputes PPh 21 under Pasal 17 in December, and also in the masa an employee stops working: the year through that month
less the TER already withheld. Pajak Masa (after 2026-10-07-pph21-december) applied TER to a leaver's final month. TER over-withholds
someone who leaves mid-year, so the estimate was wrong in exactly the month the accountant has to settle with the employee, often as a
refund.

## Spec
- [x] January–November: an employee whose `leftOn` falls in the masa leaves the TER table and goes into *Berhenti bulan ini*. The same
  `pph21Annual` runs for the months worked through the masa, less TER of the months before. The estimate is TER for the rest plus that.
- [x] The note says so, and a negative estimate reads *lebih potong Rp … (dikembalikan ke karyawan)*. The Excel adds the leaver's row.
- [x] accounting-rules 5j.

**Gate-reopeners:** none.

  TER Jan–May Rp 9 jt → last masa lebih potong Rp 5,55 jt; in July he is gone. The e2e shows his row on June's Pajak Masa.
